About

Two disciplines, one hand.

I'm Md. Zarif Hossain, an undergraduate student in the Department of Life Sciences, majoring in Microbiology at Independent University, Bangladesh (IUB). Outside the lab, I'm a self-taught graphic designer and illustrator — the same discipline that keeps an experiment reproducible is what keeps a crosshatch consistent panel to panel.

My work moves across two lanes: dense, high-contrast character illustration built entirely from ink and negative space, and event & brand graphics — posters, key art, and social visuals designed for real university clubs and live events.
