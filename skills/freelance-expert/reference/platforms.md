# Where to find work, by category

General rule: pick platforms where the *client base* matches the service,
finish the profile 100% before applying anywhere (incomplete profiles get
filtered out by both algorithms and humans), and get the first few jobs
at a fair-but-competitive price to earn reviews — reviews matter more than
rate for the first 5–10 jobs.

## Article & content writing

| Platform | What to optimize | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork | Profile title = specific niche ("SaaS SEO blog writer"), 2–3 portfolio samples, proposals within first hour of posting | Broad, high competition — niche down to stand out |
| Contently / ClearVoice / Skyword | Application + writing samples upfront | Higher-paying, brand-content focused, slower to get in |
| Direct outreach (newsletters, niche B2B blogs, SaaS companies) | Cold email with 1–2 relevant samples and a specific pitch idea for their blog | Best margins, most effort per lead |
| Medium / LinkedIn / Substack (build-in-public) | Consistent posting in one niche | Builds inbound leads over months, not a fast channel |
| Fiverr | Gig titles matching exact buyer search terms ("SEO article writing 1000 words") | Good for productized, fixed-scope offers |

## Website design / development

| Platform | What to optimize | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork | Portfolio with live links (not just screenshots), specialize by platform (Webflow/Shopify/WordPress/custom) | Clients search by tech stack |
| Fiverr | Tiered gig packages (Basic/Standard/Premium) with clear scope per tier | Buyers compare packages side by side |
| Toptal / Braintrust | Vetting/screening process, strong portfolio required upfront | Higher rates, harder entry bar |
| Webflow/Shopify/WordPress expert marketplaces (official partner directories) | Certifications, showcase sites | Platform-specific credibility signal |
| Local business outreach (cold email/LinkedIn to businesses with outdated sites) | Before/after mockup of *their* actual site as the pitch | High close rate, more manual prospecting |

## Other online jobs

| Service | Platform | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Virtual assistant | Upwork, Fancy Hands, Belay, PA/EA-specific boards | Long-term retainer roles common — vet for scope creep upfront |
| Data entry / research | Upwork, Clickworker, Scale AI/data-labeling platforms | Often high-volume, lower per-task rate — speed and accuracy both matter |
| Transcription/captioning | Rev, GoTranscript, Upwork | Style guide/accuracy tests gate entry on most of these |
| Social media management | Upwork, direct outreach to local businesses | Retainer-based; ask for brand guide + posting cadence upfront |
| Graphic design | 99designs, Dribbble (inbound), Fiverr, Upwork | Dribbble is portfolio-first, inbound leads over time |
| Translation/localization | ProZ, Upwork, TranslatorsCafe | Language-pair + subject-matter specialization pays more |
| Voiceover/audio | Voices.com, Fiverr, Upwork | Demo reel quality matters more than platform choice |

## Profile checklist (any platform)

- Photo, headline, and bio all state the specific outcome delivered, not
  just the skill list.
- 2–3 portfolio pieces minimum, each with one line of context (client type,
  goal, result) — not just the raw file/link.
- Rate set competitively for the platform's going range in that category —
  check 5–10 comparable profiles before setting it.
- Availability and response time kept accurate; fast first response wins
  jobs on every one of these platforms.
