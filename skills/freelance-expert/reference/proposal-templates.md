# Proposal, pitch, and gig-listing templates

Fill every bracket with a real specific pulled from the job post or brief.
Delete any line that would otherwise stay generic — a shorter tailored
proposal beats a longer generic one.

## Upwork/Freelancer-style proposal (article/content writing)

```
Hi [client name or "there" if unknown],

[One line referencing something specific from their post — the topic,
their industry, or a detail about their audience.]

For [specific deliverable, e.g. "the 1500-word SEO article on X"], I'd:
1. [Specific step — e.g. "target the keyword [X] and structure around the
   3 questions people actually search alongside it"]
2. [Specific step — e.g. "pull in 2-3 current examples from [their space]"]
3. [Delivery format/timeline — e.g. "deliver as a Google Doc, ready to
   publish, within [N] days"]

[One relevant sample link or a one-line credential, e.g. "Here's a similar
piece I wrote for a [industry] client: [link]"]

[One clarifying question about scope, e.g. "Quick question — do you have
a target keyword already, or should I research one?"]

[Name]
```

## Proposal (website design/build)

```
Hi [client name],

[One line acknowledging their specific goal — e.g. "Saw you're looking to
replace your current site to get more booking requests from mobile."]

Plan for this:
1. [e.g. "Quick audit of your current site's mobile experience — I noticed
   [specific issue] when I checked it."]
2. [e.g. "New layout: hero with your booking CTA above the fold, services
   section, testimonials, contact."]
3. [Platform + timeline, e.g. "Built in Webflow so you can edit copy
   yourself after launch. First draft in [N] days."]

[Link to 1-2 relevant portfolio pieces, ideally same industry or same
platform.]

[Clarifying question, e.g. "Do you have existing brand colors/logo files,
or should I start from your current site's palette?"]

[Name]
```

## Cold outreach (no job post — direct pitch)

```
Subject: [Specific observation about their site/content] + quick idea

Hi [name],

[One sentence showing you actually looked at their business — a specific
page, a specific gap, a specific competitor comparison.]

[One sentence with a concrete idea — not "I can help with your marketing"
but "your blog hasn't posted since [month] — a weekly post targeting
[specific topic] could pull in [specific audience]."]

I put together a quick example: [attach/link a short spec sample built
for them specifically — a mock headline, a mini mockup, a sample intro
paragraph].

Worth a 15-minute call to see if it's useful?

[Name]
```

## Fiverr-style gig listing

```
Title: [Exact buyer search phrase] — e.g. "I will write SEO-optimized
blog articles for [niche]"

Description:
- What you get: [specific deliverable, word count, format]
- Who it's for: [specific audience/niche]
- Process: [research → draft → 1 revision → delivery, or equivalent]
- Turnaround: [specific days]

Packages:
Basic — [narrow scope, e.g. "1 article, 500 words"] — [price]
Standard — [e.g. "1 article, 1000 words + basic SEO"] — [price]
Premium — [e.g. "1 article, 1500 words + SEO + meta description +
1 revision round"] — [price]
```

## Rules for all of the above

- Never claim a result you didn't produce ("increased traffic 300%")
  unless it's real and verifiable.
- Never copy another freelancer's sample work as your own portfolio piece.
- One proposal, one job — don't mass-send the same unedited text; the
  bracketed lines above exist specifically to force per-job tailoring.
